# Plan: #83 Devices (panels and inverters) with serial numbers

Parent #22. Scope: panels and inverters attached to an Installation: add, list, replace, replacement history.
Out of scope: strings / panel layout (#84), fault reporting (#128), order/receive workflow of components.

## Domain (docs/bpmn/mapovanie-entit.md: `Component` panel/invertor, serial numbers #83)
`InstallationDevice` (table `installation_devices`), one row per physical unit (one row per panel).
- `Type` (`Panel`, `Inverter`), `SerialNumber` (trim + upper case, max 100, **globally unique** incl. replaced devices),
  `Manufacturer` (req, 100), `Model` (req, 100), `PowerW` (req, > 0), `Status` (`Installed`, `Replaced`).
- Audit: `RegisteredBy`, `RegisteredAt`; replacement: `RemovedAt`, `RemovedBy`, `RemovalReason`, `ReplacedByDeviceId`; new device has `ReplacesDeviceId`.
- History is preserved: a replaced device is never deleted, only marked `Replaced` and linked to its successor.
- Replacement keeps the type; the new device has its own serial/manufacturer/model/power.
- Rules: add only while installation is not `HandedOver` (replacement stays possible after handover: warranty/service);
  replace only an `Installed` device; unique index on `ReplacesDeviceId` makes a double replacement race a 409.
- Customer accounts read only devices of their own installations (via `ICustomerScope`, other = 404). Writes: `InstallationRoles.Field`.
